Privacy policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

MyAutomatedChannel uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel it uploads to.

What it accesses

YouTube:

  • Permission to upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to the channel’s name and ID, to confirm which channel is connected (youtube.readonly).
  • Instagram: publishing Reels to the owner’s account (instagram_basic, instagram_content_publishing) through the owner’s Facebook Page.
  • TikTok: the owner’s basic profile (user.info.basic) and uploading or posting the owner’s videos (video.upload, video.publish).

How data is stored and used

  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner’s own computer and is used only to upload the owner’s videos.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Facebook/Instagram in Facebook settings → Apps and websites, TikTok in TikTok settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
